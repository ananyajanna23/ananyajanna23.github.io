About Me

Janna Ananya

Financial Educator & Money Clarity Coach
PGDM Banking & Finance Fintech Experience FP&A Experience Finance Content Creator For Professionals

I'm a financial educator on a mission to make personal finance accessible and clear. After working in fintech and FP&A, I realized most professionals feel lost when it comes to their own money—not because they're not smart enough, but because financial concepts are explained in unnecessarily complex ways.

So I'm here to change that. I don't give investment advice or tell you what portfolio to build. Instead, I help you understand *how* financial concepts work, ask the right questions, and make decisions that align with your values and goals.

If you're tired of financial jargon and want to actually understand your money, let's talk.
